MODEL TRANSPARENCY

How the engine thinks

No mystery score. Every projection is built from traceable inputs and explicit assumptions.

01

Discover

The app enumerates active sports, events and DFS player-prop markets instead of relying on a tiny hard-coded list.

02

Normalize

PrizePicks, Underdog, Dabble and Pick6 lines are normalized into a common player / market / threshold schema.

03

De-vig

Sportsbook Over/Under prices are converted to implied probabilities and the bookmaker margin is removed.

04

Infer

Each fair market probability is mapped to an implied statistical mean using a volatility model. Sharp books receive modestly higher weight.

05

Ensemble

The weighted implied means create a consensus projection. Market disagreement lowers confidence.

06

Score

The DFS threshold is compared with the model mean to estimate More/Less probability, standardized edge and an interpretable confidence score.

Production upgrade path

The included engine is deliberately auditable. For a serious production model, calibrate market-specific variance from historical outcomes, layer in injuries/lineups/weather/pace/opponent efficiency, and backtest probability calibration by sport and stat. The architecture is designed so those models can replace or blend with the market-consensus baseline.
